Zombie Scrolling

Cellphone addiction can make us scroll for hours without noticing the time. It can affect our sleep, focus, and time with family and friends.

Take short breaks from your phone and give yourself time to rest and study without a screen.

Set a limit for social media and avoid using your phone when you are about to sleep.

Simple Ways to Cope

Turn off unnecessary notifications, put your phone away while studying, and spend more time doing hobbies or talking with people.

You can also learn more about healthy screen habits through this resource.

Remember

Your phone is a useful tool, but it should not control your time. Learn to put it down and enjoy the real world around you.
