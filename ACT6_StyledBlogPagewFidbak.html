Pax Silica What!?

So, what exactly is Pax Silica, and why should Filipinos pay attention to it? Behind the plans for technology, industries, and investments are bigger questions about what this initiative could mean for our economy, our communities, and the natural environment around us.

According to GMA News, the Philippines and the United States are planning a 4,000-acre industrial hub in the Luzon Economic Corridor under the Pax Silica initiative. The project is connected to advanced manufacturing, critical minerals, semiconductors, and artificial intelligence. While these developments may open new opportunities, they also invite an important question: how do we balance progress with environmental protection? GMA News

Pax Silica: A Chance to Grow?

According to BCDA President and CEO Joshua Bingcang, Pax Silica could bring major opportunities to the Philippines, including new investments and more than 130,000 potential high-quality jobs. It could also strengthen industries such as semiconductors, artificial intelligence, advanced manufacturing, and critical minerals. Philippine Information Agency

What Can We Get?

  • More jobs for Filipinos
  • More foreign investments
  • Growth in AI and semiconductors
  • Better skills and training
  • More local mineral processing
  • More chances for local businesses

But What Could It Cost?

While Pax Silica promises jobs and development, many Filipinos are also asking what might happen to the environment, water, communities, and livelihoods. The proposed hub covers around 1,600 hectares in New Clark City, Tarlac, raising concerns about land, water use, and possible displacement of farmers and Indigenous communities. Philstar.com

But not everyone agrees. Some Filipinos welcome the opportunities, while others worry about its impact on nature, communities, and livelihoods.
