8 Steps to Start a Business

1. Find Your Business Idea

-Start with a product or service that solves a problem or meets a need.

2. Know Your Market

-Learn who your customers are and what they are looking for.

3. Create a Business Plan

-Get your goals and decide how your business will operate and grow.

4. Plan Your Budget

-Know your startup costs and make sure you have enough funds.

5. Choose a Business Name

-Pick a name that is simple, memorable, and fits your brand.

6. Handle Legal Requirements

-Prepare the necessary registrations, permits, and licenses.

7. Build and Promote Your Business

-Create your product advertisement and post it to social media platform to attract customers and client.

8. Launch and Keep Improving

-Start small, serve your customers well, and learn from their feedback.
